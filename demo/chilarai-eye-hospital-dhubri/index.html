Understanding cataract

When the world starts to look faded.

A cataract is a clouding of the eye’s natural lens. It usually develops slowly, and many people notice it only when daily tasks get harder.

Glare or halos around lights at night
Blurred, cloudy or dim vision
Colours look faded or yellowish

These can have other causes too. Only an eye examination can tell — book one if you notice changes.

Questions

Good to know before you come.

Is the Ayushman card accepted?

Yes. Chilarai Eye Hospital is empanelled under Ayushman Bharat (PM‑JAY). Bring your Ayushman card and Aadhaar, and ask the desk what is covered for your treatment.

Do you do cataract surgery?

Cataract surgery is among the procedures listed for the hospital, including cataract surgery for children. An eye examination comes first to decide what is right for you.

Can I bring my child for an eye check-up?

Yes. The hospital’s listed procedures include children’s eye care. Choose “Child” in the booking form above.

What should I bring?

Your current glasses, any old prescriptions or eye reports, a list of medicines you take, and your Ayushman card if you have one.

Where is the hospital?

On R.K. Mission Road in College Nagar, Dhubri, near R.K. Mission School. The entrance is wheelchair-accessible.
