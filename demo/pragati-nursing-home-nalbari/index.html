Questions

Good to know.

Is the Ayushman card accepted?

Yes. Pragati Nursing Home is empanelled under Ayushman Bharat (PM‑JAY). Bring your Ayushman card and Aadhaar and ask the desk what is covered.

What is Diacare?

Diacare is the nursing home’s diabetes-care service — check-ups, monitoring and long-term care with a consultant diabetologist.

Do you handle pregnancy and delivery?

Obstetrics and gynaecology, along with newborn and children’s care, are among the nursing home’s services. Call to talk about your pregnancy care.

I’m an Assam Healthcare Co-op member. Do I get a discount?

According to the co-op’s listing, members get 10% off laboratory services and 5% off medicines. Show your membership at the desk.

Where is the nursing home?

On the Nalbari–Hajo–Guwahati Road at Santipur (Hajo Road), Nalbari. Tap “Directions” above to open Google Maps.
