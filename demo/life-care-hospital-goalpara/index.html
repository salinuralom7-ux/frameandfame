General medicine

Your first stop when someone falls ill.

General medicine is Life Care Hospital’s listed speciality. A physician examines the patient, orders the tests needed in the hospital’s own lab and radiology, and plans treatment — with the ICU and operation theatre in the same building if more care is needed.

Questions

Good to know.

Is the Ayushman card accepted?

Yes. Life Care Hospital is empanelled under Ayushman Bharat (PM‑JAY). Bring your Ayushman card and Aadhaar, and ask the desk what is covered.

Does the hospital have an ICU?

Yes, ICU services are available. Call the hospital to ask about bed availability.

Can I get tests done at the same place?

Yes. The hospital has its own laboratory and radiology services.

Where is the hospital?

On Agia Road, Udaynagar, Goalpara. Tap “Directions” above to open Google Maps.
