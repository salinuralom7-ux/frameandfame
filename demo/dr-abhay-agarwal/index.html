Walk, work and play again — without pain holding you back.

Dr. Abhay Agarwal is an orthopaedic surgeon in Guwahati with nine years in healthcare. Alongside his MS in Orthopaedics, he holds a diploma in sports medicine — so whether it is a footballer’s torn ligament or a parent’s aching knee, the aim is the same: a clear diagnosis and a plan that gets you moving.

He consults at Excel Care Hospital on NH-37, Paschim Boragaon, and sees patients in English, Hindi and Assamese.

Questions

Before you visit.

What is the consultation fee?

The consultation fee is ₹700. Call the clinic if you have questions about follow-up visits.

What should I bring?

Bring any X-rays, MRI or CT scans, blood reports and a list of medicines you take. Wear loose clothing so the painful joint can be examined easily.

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Book ahead so your time is confirmed.
