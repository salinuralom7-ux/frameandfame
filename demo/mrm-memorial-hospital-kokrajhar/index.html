Questions

Before you come in.

Does the hospital accept the Ayushman card?

Yes. M.R.M Memorial Hospital is empanelled under Ayushman Bharat (PM‑JAY). Bring your Ayushman card and Aadhaar, and ask the help desk what is covered for your treatment.

Is there an ICU?

Yes, the hospital has ICU services. Call the hospital to ask about bed availability.

Can I get my blood tests done here?

Yes. The hospital has its own laboratory, and radiology services too.

Where exactly is the hospital?

On J.D. Road, Ward No. 4, opposite Hotel Omm Shree, Kokrajhar. Tap “Get directions” above to open it in Google Maps.
