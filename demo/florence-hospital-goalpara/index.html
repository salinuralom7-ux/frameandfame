About the hospital

A Goalpara hospital, for Goalpara families.

Florence Hospital is a private hospital on Pancha Ratna Road. It brings emergency care, an intensive care unit, surgery and in-house diagnostics together, so patients and families are not sent from place to place.

Since 2018 the hospital has been empanelled under Ayushman Bharat (PM‑JAY), so eligible patients can ask about cashless treatment.

Questions

Before you visit.

Do you accept the Ayushman card?

Yes. Florence Hospital is empanelled under Ayushman Bharat (PM‑JAY). Bring your Ayushman card and Aadhaar, and ask the desk what is covered for your treatment.

Is there an ICU?

Yes, the hospital has ICU services. Call to ask about bed availability.

Can tests be done at the hospital?

Yes. Florence Hospital has its own laboratory and radiology services.

Which number should I call?

Try the main line first. If it is busy, call the second line.
