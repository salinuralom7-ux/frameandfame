Questions

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. You are billed monthly, with no lock-in. If it stops being worth it, you stop.

Who pays for the ads?

Your ad budget goes from you straight to Meta, on your own ad account. Our fee is separate, and every rupee of ad spend is shown in your monthly report.

Who shoots the videos?

Both of us. We write the script and direct; your own staff film most of it to our brief, and we come in for the shoots that need us. Travel beyond 60 km is agreed separately.

Will you also work with my competitor?

Not in your district while you are with us. We never run campaigns for two businesses chasing the same customers.

How many enquiries will we get?

We won't promise a number before we have seen your market. On the call we will tell you honestly what is realistic for your town and budget.
